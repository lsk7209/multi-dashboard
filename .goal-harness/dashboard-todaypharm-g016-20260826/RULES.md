# Rules

1. Do not declare completion without tests or equivalent validation.
2. Do not break existing working behavior.
3. Do not make unrequested broad rewrites.
4. Do not leave temporary code, dummy logic, or TODO-only work as final.
5. Do not expose secrets.
6. Do not change production DBs, live servers, or deploy targets without existing explicit authority.
7. Add no dependency without a recorded reason.
8. Record risky or production-facing operations before execution.
9. Do not hide failed tests or collector gaps.
10. Preserve unrelated generated and untracked user files.
11. Keep AdSense, GSC, IndexNow and Vercel API/CLI mutations out of scope.
